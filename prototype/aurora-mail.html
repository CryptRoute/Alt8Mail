About this concept

What you are looking at, and what carries into the skin.

Aurora is an enterprise mail workspace built on Outlook's four-zone layout, with a tenant identity, a contextual command bar, security and classification cues, a working directory and calendar, and a reading pane designed for long threads. The layout collapses to a two-pane tablet view and a single-pane phone view. Meridian, the people and the mail are placeholders — your organization name and logo drop into the same slots.
