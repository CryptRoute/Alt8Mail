About Alt8Mail

One secure workspace for email, calendars, tasks and files.

Alt8Mail brings your inbox, calendar, tasks and files together in one focused workspace. AI assistance helps you summarize long conversations, draft a reply in your own voice, translate messages and surface what needs attention — without sending your mail anywhere it should not go. Advanced security runs quietly in the background: passkeys and two-factor sign-in, phishing and impersonation checks, SPF/DKIM/DMARC verification, attachment scanning, encrypted transport, server-side rules and a complete audit trail.

AI that works with youSummaries, reply drafts and translation stay inside your workspace, with tenant-controlled AI settings.
Security by defaultPasskeys, TOTP, app passwords, sender authentication and malware scanning protect every sign-in and message.
Your mail, anywhereReal IMAP, POP3 and SMTP access works with Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird and any standards-based client.
Server-backed · API responses are never cached.

Connect another email client

Use these settings in Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird or any standard IMAP, POP3 or SMTP client.

Sign in to load your server settings.
  1. Add an account. In your email app choose Add account → Other (or IMAP), then enter your name and full Alt8Mail address.
  2. Choose the protocol. IMAP is recommended so folders and read state stay in sync across devices. Choose POP3 only if you deliberately want mail downloaded to one device.
  3. Enter the server details. Use your full email address as the username and your normal mailbox password. Turn on SSL/TLS and require authentication for outgoing mail.
  4. Using two-factor authentication? Create an app password in Settings → Security & privacy and use that instead of your sign-in password.
  5. Test both directions. Send a message to another address and ask for a reply. If sending fails, confirm that your network allows outbound SMTP on port 587.

Use the full email address as the username. Your administrator can confirm the exact hostname if your organization uses a custom mail domain.
