## Operational notes

- The tenant policy must set `.encryption.mode` to `server_managed` and
  `.encryption.protocol` to `openpgp` before the APIs enable key setup.
- `ALT8MAIL_CRYPTO_SECRET` is generated by `install.sh` in
  `/opt/a8m/secrets/crypto_secret` and exported through `app.env`.
- Encrypted messages store an empty body and crypto metadata; decryption
  fetches the raw IMAP message on demand. If the raw source is unavailable
  (IMAP disabled or a synthesized source), decryption returns a conflict until
  Phase 2 adds ciphertext storage.
- The Sent copy remains in the server-managed store; the recipient receives
  PGP/MIME ciphertext.
